# Session 25 -- Khantor TTRPG Session Notes

## 1. Setting & World Context

**Locations: the refinery hallway of the ghost iron mine, the ravine and ridge road below the mine (Zhar's route), the mine's entrance chambers, the woods between the mine and Cross Hollow, and the square in Cross Hollow.** The session picks up mid-fight with Harlow's face turned into Malas Vance's. Vance bargains with Rhiaah, holds Doreth Kell in place, and unmakes him with a wave of grey mist that also drops Rhiaah and cracks the sonic refiner. He takes Rhiaah's coin and the fire in Malek's blade, warns Malek that Kell will be back, and walks out whistling. {Caly frenzies and Malek knocks her out.}[^r1] Meanwhile Momo reaches Zhar at the Nightline and leads him to the mine, where he cuts down a gunman and a Blackheart captain holding a boy hostage, and meets Yorg Hardmud. The party regroups, rests while Caly rides out her frenzy, and walks the freed miners through the black woods to Cross Hollow. There they kill Floyd Gravel, Hark Thirstrun and the last of the Blackheart in the square, free the magistrate, and settle in for the night with the town. Next session the party heads back to Fort Ashby.

### New World Facts Established

- {**Call of the Void.** Vance's grey mist}[^r3] unmakes what it touches. Rhiaah's prosthetics tarnished and rusted, the ghost iron in the refinery turned a copper-grey, the walls chipped and sanded away, and Kell flaked and melted to dust. Vance pulled the dust back to his hand and pressed it into a milky white stone, which he pocketed.
- **Doreth Kell will return.** Vance says Kell will be back with a score to settle with Malek. "He may not look the same next time, but it'll be him."
- **Revenant regeneration.** {Caly studied Kell's wounds knitting shut (Medicine 19)}[^r2]. It is a property of Kell's kind of revenant, not of the Revenant species. Caly can simulate it by spending Hit Dice, and nothing is beyond discovery as she grows.
- **Caly's frenzy.** Caly remembers everything that happens while she is in a frenzy. Her friends can try to talk her out of it, but the DC is beyond them for now. It will get easier as she becomes comfortable with her nature. She can wake already in a frenzy, which makes sleeping around her dangerous. Her body does not knit and heal like a living one: her wrists were stripped raw where the bindings held her.
- **The black wood.** The trees around Cross Hollow are living, growing charcoal, adapted to the area's constant lightning strikes. The wood is an incredibly durable fuel that burns for a very long time. The survivors' camp burned it in their boiler.
- **Corpse flowers.** There are more of them than the one in the ravine. They eat the dead, which is part of why revenants seldom come to the Hollow Reach.
- **The mine's guards.** Everyone in the mine was starving. The Blackheart guards were better fed than the miners but still rationed.
- **Burnside.** A tall man with a short-trimmed silvering beard. He left the mine a couple of weeks ago with six carts of worked ghost iron and a good number of his men, and he has not come back. The regular shipments stopped with him.
- **Getting in and out.** Rattlespur's delegations came rarely. People used to come over from Sawtooth Crossing, but rumour says a mudslide blocked the pass. A road over the pass southwest of Cross Hollow drops down into the Badlands. The mountain route the miners came in by runs past giants (who have a whole city out there) and small mechanical creatures. One of the miners' escorts traded away tools from Copper Waystation to a giant.
- **The magistrate's account.** The magistrate has been a captive for 10 years. The Nightline went down roughly 15 years ago and cut Cross Hollow off from the rest of the Iron Marches. Burnside forced him to lie to the nation about the town: officially, the region has seen a rise in ghoul births, which is why the dead are piled on the mound and not buried. When outsiders came, the Blackheart cleaned him up and had him repeat the lie, and they threatened his daughter to keep him in line. He wears dirty, ruffled Greystone Coalition robes.
- **Grady owns Fort Ashby.** Yorg has heard that Grady Ash won Fort Ashby in a game years ago, and that he owns the whole town, the Mercy House, and the region around it. Rhiaah has known for a while.
- **Dustwater's reservoir.** Dustwater is famous in the Iron Marches for a recent invention: a reservoir holding huge amounts of water on the surface. Most of the region's water is pumped up from deep aquifers, and Caly has never seen a reservoir in the Iron Marches, though Ixaltan has them (History 21). Pops has mentioned an accomplished inventor in Dustwater, and a Nox Rider inventor there.
- **Yorg Hardmud.** A dwarven barbarian who has been captive in the mine. He and Kenneth worked their way north through the Red Valley, trained in Ixaltan, and build pumps. They were heading to Dustwater to meet Yorg's cousin, a hydrologist who works on the reservoir there. Yorg rages too. "I give in. I just stop caring."
- **The god of war.** The party has heard him named three ways: Awu (by Quessa Merrow in the bar), a giant name that translates to "war king" (in the giants' history), and Ares (in the Axiom). Rhiaah's warhammer was forged by him.
- **Cross Hollow's people.** Most are outcasts and pariahs who came up into these hills to settle, brought their families, and stayed. The hunter Maurice says Rattlespur never did anything for the town and wrote it off when travel got hard.
- **Time.** About 15 minutes pass for each short rest. The fight in Cross Hollow ends in the evening of the same day.

## 3. Scene-by-Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: Now, Now, Friends (~00:00--00:07)

Kell holds Rhiaah's silver coin, and Malek lies on his back bleeding from his ears, eyes and mouth. Harlow steps through the door he fled through, his face melting into Vance's, and {spreads his palms like a salesman}[^r4]. "Now, now, friends. Let's not spoil a fine afternoon." {Vance rolls a 26 on initiative.}[^r5]

He turns to Rhiaah: "What is it that you want? And I hope you're willing to pay the price." Rhiaah starts to answer that a friend told her there is a dangerous place he needs her to go, and Kell shouts, "Enough of this!" and steps toward her. Vance spends his action on Hold Monster. Kell fails the save (17) and seizes in place, {his eyes burning with hatred}[^r6]. "You were saying?"

{Rhiaah tells him there is a task he needs done}[^r7], and she wants to be at the front of that expedition. "And of course, I wouldn't want to sour my relationship with my favorite tea-drinking friend." Caly uses her healbot to cast Cure Wounds on Malek (11). Vance looks over at Rhiaah: "We really are going to have to talk about trade secrets."

### Scene 2: Momo's Errand (~00:07--00:41)

Some time earlier, Zhar sits hidden in the wrecked front of the Nightline, keeping watch over the crater while the Modron work inside. Momo bursts out of the far tree line at full speed, runs up his leg onto his shoulder and makes a racket of radio static. "I do not understand you, Momo." Momo jumps down, bites the edge of his shoe, tugs, and runs.

Zhar follows him past the abandoned survivors' camp and around the mound of corpses. Momo gives the smoke beyond the mound a wide berth and heads for a ravine. At the edge (Perception 18), Zhar smells swamp, soft wet decay he has not smelled since Tareth. The ravine runs about 150 feet across, as if the forest had cracked and dropped. Across it sits a rustic wooden town with despondent people and the occasional armed man walking the streets. Zhar is on the side closer to the Pale Dominion, and he ignores the town.

Momo leads north-northeast until the mountains rise and the ravine narrows. Zhar picks him up and climbs down. At the bottom he points in different directions, and Momo squeaks only when he points at the far wall (Animal Handling 14). Zhar climbs out behind a big boulder (Survival 11) and sneaks around it (nat 20). There are no woods here. A road climbs the ridge, where nothing grows and the ground is grey and beaten hard, past a split boulder and on up. Momo did not lead him to the town. The party had told Zhar they were going to Cross Hollow, and he did not know about the mine.

Halfway up the northern face, the road opens onto a flattened clearing with room for carts and a black-timber doorway set into the peak. As Zhar steps out, two armed men in dusters and hats come running out of the doorway. He reads them (Insight 21): one stows his pistol so he can run faster, and both are fleeing something. Zhar curls into cover and they run past without seeing him. Approaching the door (Perception 15), he hears metal on metal, a distant echoing report that might be a gunshot, then a high-pitched whine accelerating into a reverberating thump. He goes in.

About 20 feet in, the granite walls begin to show dull grey striations. The mine is lit by torches. The first chamber, about 30 by 60 feet, is an all-out brawl among roughly 15 people, with tipped carts of grey rock, guns, swords, axes, pickaxes, shovels and barrels. One man beats another with a set of chains. Zhar cannot tell the sides apart (Insight 10) until a man in a duster draws a pistol and shoots a gaunt, ragged man of about 25 dead. "Okay, yeah, I'm going after that guy." Zhar hits twice (22 for 9, Nick 17 for 14) and cuts across his stomach. Momo bites and misses. The gunman turns his pistol on Zhar at point-blank range (14), and Zhar parries it up and away so the shot goes into the ceiling.

### Scene 3: {Call of the Void}[^r12] (~00:41--00:50)

Back in the hallway, Malek tells Vance that friends do not keep secrets. Vance asks Rhiaah what she would have him do. "I'm doing what you asked. I have not yet fulfilled my bargain." He asks whether she wants this creature destroyed. Rhiaah tells him Kell is already gone, a shell. Malek adds that removing him from the premises would be the right course of action.

Vance calls the void. A grey mist pours from his raised hand and fills the chamber. He chooses not to affect the party as much: they save with advantage (19, 19, 18) and take half, 7 each. Rhiaah, at 6 HP, drops. Kell fails (17) and takes 97 force damage. Rhiaah's gears stop and her heart slows, and she drops to a knee and falls on her side. Her prosthetics tarnish and rust. Kell flakes, peels and melts to dust. The ghost iron in the room tarnishes to a copper-grey, the cracked sonic refiner that shot Malek gets a crack along its casing and winds down, and the rock chips and sands away. Then Vance pulls the dust back to himself, all that growth and life, into a small sphere. To Malek's horror, it solidifies into a milky white stone, which Vance clutches and pockets. Kell turns to look at Vance in horror.

### Scene 4: The Last of Kell (~00:50--01:10)

Caly is entering a frenzy, and the DM tells her Vance is the source of it. She spends a Hope to hold it off for one round. Her eyes stay on Rhiaah, and she rushes to her sister and heals her. As a bonus action she studies how Kell regenerated (Medicine 19). Then, knowing the frenzy is coming, she retreats into a corner, away from everyone.

At the end of her turn Vance draws a ghost iron revolver and fires twice into Kell. Kell's wounds partly knit, and with nowhere left to go he lunges at Vance with two slams (dirty 20 and 21). Vance steps lightly to one side, then the other, deflects a clawed hand, and gently pushes him away.

The DM gives Malek a Hope as the brand on his arm cracks and bleeds, and he sees the Iron Brand on Kell alight and cracking further across the Order of Mending mark. Malek drinks his last potion and points his weapon at Kell. Eldritch Blast lances across the room (17). In Vance's presence the magic mixes with his, and dinner-plate holes churn to sand and slough off Kell, spinning around Vance. Vance reaches out with Unmaking Touch (28). Kell howls in rage as his body comes apart into the swirling sand. Vance reaches into what is left of him, takes the coin, and says, "I'll hold on to this until you settle up."

### Scene 5: The Boy at Gunpoint (~01:10--01:29)

Zhar finishes the gunman (17, 9 damage), straight through the chest. Gunfire echoes from deep in the mine, and screaming comes from the next chamber. Momo is out of range of Rhiaah. Zhar looks for anyone who is not gaunt and dirty, but everyone here is starving (Perception). The guards are better fed but still rationed. Both sides are fighting for their lives. These are the miners who forced their way to the entrance, held at a last stand. Zhar cannot tell friend from foe (Survival 14) and pushes on to find his friends.

In the next chamber the aggressor is clear. A mob of emaciated miners with pickaxes, shovels, chair legs, metal bars and rocks advances, some with broken chains on their arms. A single emaciated dwarf leads them with a chain in his hands, his beard roughly shaved in places. Between Zhar and the mob, three men in dusters push heavy carts toward the exit while a fourth, the captain, holds a boy of 15 to 18 at gunpoint. "Get back, I'll kill the boy! You lot, get that out!"

Zhar rolls a nat 20 on Perception for initiative, closes the distance unseen, Hunter's Marks the captain and stabs him with Nick on another nat 20, for 32. Out of character, Xylus: "Zhar really doesn't like when people fuck with kids." Zhar's transformation sets off his Unnerving Aura. One guard fails, one passes on a 19, and the captain passes on an 18. The mob roars forward. The dwarf leaps onto a table to engage four guards bare-fisted. The DM confirms he is a barbarian. He lashes his chain around one guard's throat, pulls him down and headbutts him unconscious.

The captain lets go of the boy and swings his scimitar at Zhar twice. He misses the first, and on the second he bumps the cart, heavy with refined ghost iron. Two guards dogpile the dwarf, who is beaten down but not about to fall.

### Scene 6: Vance Settles Up (~01:29--01:37)

Vance walks over to Malek, looks down at the device and nudges it with his foot. Broken. Then he summons Malek's own blade into his hand, burning, wills the flame (the aether it absorbed from Vesper) out of the blade and into his palm, and hands the blade back. "Thank you for this. It shall be useful in the coming years." He glances at the spot where Kell was. "You know that one will be back, right? And now he's got a score to settle with you. He may not look the same next time, but it'll be him." Then: "Careful what friends you keep. Tell Luna I'll be in touch." He walks out whistling.

Caly's frenzy takes her. She punches Rhiaah for 2. "No Caly, it's nap time." Malek Pommel Strikes her (dirty 20) for 8, one short of her 9 HP. She hisses at him, and he applies Iron Brand for the last point to knock her out, then removes it. Rhiaah ties her up (Dexterity 20). "Of course I rolled that 20 for tying up my sister."

### Scene 7: Kenneth Sent Me (~01:37--02:09)

In the second chamber, Zhar misses all three attacks (1, 2, 6). The dwarf grabs a ghost iron ingot, jumps a guard, cold-clocks him and keeps punching him with it. The guards attack the dwarf at disadvantage, and one hits for 6. The captain swings at Zhar, a nat 1 and a 21, for 6 slashing.

Malek and Rhiaah carry Caly back through the refinery: the device cracked, the ingots tarnished, the walls pockmarked and peeled. The armory beyond is nearly empty, a sword here and a disassembled pistol there. A man in broken chains grabs a sword off the wall and charges out. In the armory Rhiaah's link to Momo snaps back, and through his eyes she sees a scimitar swinging down, a spilled cart of ghost iron, the dwarf pummeling someone with an ingot, and a braid with a piece of bone cut into it. Zhar's here.

Zhar hits the captain for 11, bloodying him, misses once, then lands the last attack. The captain's head comes off and rolls onto the pile of ghost iron, and his hot blood steams on the metal. The dwarf roars into a reckless attack, caves in one guard's head, shoves the corpse into the last one and drop-kicks him in the crotch. Then he looks at Zhar: "She said you'd come." "Kenneth sent me to get you." "Your friends are in there causing a ruckus." The miners overwhelm the last guards. There are losses: people broken, bleeding and dead. Zhar sees two people slip out of the mine, each carrying a brick (Perception 16). Momo races off in the opposite direction. Zhar heals the dwarf with Cure Wounds (15). The dwarf barks orders: "Make sure nobody leaves. We leave together."

Momo runs up onto Rhiaah's shoulder as the two groups meet in the hallway, Malek and Rhiaah carrying a bound and unconscious Caly. Most of the captives are unlocked, and a few were lost along the way. Zhar suggests taking everyone to Fort Ashby, where Yorg's son is. Yorg says there are 40 or 50 folk still in Cross Hollow, and the guards who fled will have gone there, though only a handful of Blackheart remain. He describes Burnside: a tall man with a short-trimmed silvering beard, who left a couple of weeks ago with carts full of iron and a lot of his men. The miners have not eaten in days. "Sometimes we wouldn't get anything to eat. They take it from us."

Rhiaah looks at the mine and says, "This thing could start wars. It could also do something better, though." Malek goes back down the hall for the device. He has no idea how it works, but a large crack runs across the tarnished casing. It is broken but still has value, and he tucks it into Caly's bag of holding. Rhiaah says what they have here could very well fix the Nightline and revitalize the region. Malek tells Rhiaah that Vance had a message for Luna: he'll be in touch. "Noted." About 35 freed miners are now in the party's charge, along with one surly dwarf.

### Scene 8: Riding It Out (~02:09--02:47)

The party takes a short rest. Malek talks quietly with the miners. Zhar bandages and stabilizes the wounded with his healer's kit. Rhiaah is out of hit dice. A few of the miners say people in town have hidden caches of food. The local wildlife "tries to eat you," Rhiaah says. "You got your ass kicked by that flower," the DM reminds Malek. "It was a tree."

Zhar borrows the binoculars and walks down to the split boulder overlook. In the square of Cross Hollow, crates are dragged out. A portly man climbs on top of them and addresses the crowd while two Blackheart shove another heavyset man to his knees in front of them. Zhar is too far to hear. On his Insight (nat 20), the crowd is subdued and not moving to do what the man says. He is threatening them, but no one is leaving.

Caly wakes, hogtied and still in a frenzy, her eyes red. She snarls at Rhiaah and fails to break the bonds. "I'm not gonna knock her out. You need to ride this out. You need to find a way to come out of this yourself." Caly snaps at Rhiaah's hand and misses, and Rhiaah stuffs her glove in Caly's mouth. "Chew on that for a bit." When Zhar returns, Rhiaah is taking apart one of Caly's contraptions in front of her, making eye contact the whole time, "testing theories." It only makes Caly angrier. She tries the bonds again (Athletics 15 against DC 23).

Zhar describes the two men. Yorg identifies the man on the crates as Floyd, who controls the town's food, and the man on his knees as the magistrate, who is not with the Blackheart. Zhar sits beside Caly to be a calm presence and rubs her back, and heals her (11). Malek takes the Pops angle and asks her whether this is what she wants Pops to see when they meet again (Persuasion 21). It does not get through, but eventually Caly regains control on her own. In her head was Kell, cackling and whispering at her to rage, and Vance destroying precious things and dropping her sister. Her eyes lighten back toward their new green. She mends the glove.

When Rhiaah unbinds her, Caly finds the flesh stripped where the bindings held. Her body does not knit and heal normally. She puts on her own gloves to cover her wrists. She remembers everything, and on Insight (14) she understands that her friends can pull her out of a frenzy, but the DC is beyond them for now and will get easier as she grows comfortable with her nature. She tells them to tie her up whenever they sleep. "I have no more need for sleep nor breath." Rhiaah says that is why she did not knock Caly out a second time. Caly suggests giving the dwarf Rhiaah's hammer for the trip to town. "What do I need a hammer for?" Malek: "I saw how effective you were with your fists. And the ingot."

### Scene 9: Through the Black Woods (~02:47--03:00)

The miners will not stay behind. "They don't want any part of this place. Not anymore." Yorg: "Fuckers cut my beard." Caly keeps toward the front of the group but apart from the miners, since she has been in two rages today. At the split boulder the party can take the road or cut through the woods. Zhar (Survival 24, advantage) judges the woods the stealthier and more direct route.

The black trees are almost stone-like. Zhar (Survival 23) works out that they are living, growing charcoal adapted to the region's constant lightning, an incredibly durable fuel. He remembers the survivors' camp burning it in their boiler. Caly asks whether he still has ingredients for the salve the old man taught him to make. He does not, but he knows what he needs, and they agree to look on the road back to Fort Ashby.

At the edge of town they hear the bassy voice of the man on the porch: "People, if you want this man released, you'll bring me what food you have. Place it in these boxes, and we will leave this place to your own devices. You get in our way, we will kill you and take your food anyway. The choice is yours."

### Scene 10: The Square at Cross Hollow (~03:00--03:59)

The party brings Yorg and three able-bodied miners and leaves the rest behind. The stealth approach down an alley fails on three nat 1s, and the Blackheart see them come out into the open 60 to 90 feet away. The Blackheart roll no initiative above 9.

Zhar uses Adrenaline Rush, Hunter's Marks Floyd (the "big fat" one on the crates) and hits him (18, 9 damage), then Nick. His Unnerving Aura frightens Floyd and both toughs beside him. The DM points out that the woman backed against a fence is the mother of the boy Malek fed. Malek recognizes the two toughs as the men who ran past Zhar on the road. "You want to take from these people, and you've already lost a mine. Get out of here and you may live with your lives" (Intimidation 18). The four nearest Blackheart are frightened of him, but after glancing at the crates at their feet they stand their ground. Malek puts his boot on a crate and summons his blade with a flourish. Caly casts True Strike and fires her pistol for a nat 20 and 15 radiant. Rhiaah shoots another tough (21, 9 damage) on her last Bladesong.

Floyd swings at Zhar out of fear, misses, and falls off the crates. Hark, whose name the party does not know, grabs the woman and holds her as a human shield. Yorg roars, runs 35 feet and swings Rhiaah's hammer across one tough's chest (13 and 11). The toughs fire back: one shot kills one of the men in the fight, a tough's nat 1 on Malek leaves the cylinder hanging out of his gun, and shots at Zhar and Yorg miss.

Zhar opens the next round on Floyd (22, 12 damage), then Nick on a nat 20, for 35. Floyd stumbles back, frightened, and Zhar takes his head clean off. It lands next to the food crates. The crowd gasps. Zhar moves Hunter's Mark to Hark and uses Adrenaline Rush again, and both remaining toughs fail the aura. The DM rules that attacks on Hark through his hostage are at disadvantage, or she takes half the damage. Malek attacks carefully at disadvantage (22) with Iron Brand for 23, and Hark lets the woman go. Caly casts True Strike again and shoots the tough standing over the magistrate through the eyes (19, 8 damage). His corpse slumps onto the magistrate, who screams. Caly shouts to Yorg to get the magistrate behind the cart. Rhiaah stares down the last tough and spends her last level 2 slot on Spellfire Lance at his friend, a silver sphere of light from the crystal in her mechanical palm, for 23 radiant. Yorg chucks the hammer at that one, runs after it, catches it and uppercuts him (11 and 12).

Hark slashes Zhar (22, 8 damage) and Malek (24, 9 damage). Malek answers with Hellish Rebuke, and Hark takes half. Two freed miners walk up and shoot Rhiaah's Lance target dead with liberated guns. ### Scene 11: Food in the Square (~03:59--04:28)

Rhiaah pulls the corpse off the magistrate, a heavyset man in dirty, ruffled Greystone Coalition robes, and offers her hand and the Nox symbol. He latches onto her and cries. "I'm here to keep you safe." Starving people, mostly women, children and those who cannot fight, come out of their homes and rush the crates, grabbing whatever food they can in something close to a riot. Zhar whistles the all-clear, and people scatter, then see their families coming in from the mine. They almost forget the food, clutching it to their chests as they run to greet loved ones they have not seen in a long time. Malek pats someone's shoulder, picks up a severed head and soccer-kicks it down the road.

Rhiaah sets the magistrate on an empty crate and sits beside him. "When you're ready." She tells him a dwarf boy sent them to save his father, and the acting marshal sent them to investigate, and that Marshal Cindergrave is dead. "That's a right shame. She was a strong woman." He has not kept up with politics. He has been a captive for 10 years. The Nightline went down roughly 15 years ago and cut Cross Hollow off. Rhiaah tells him the mine holds enough ghost iron to fix the Nightline, and enough to start a war. "That is what I was most afraid of." Burnside spoke to him at length when his men were not threatening him, and forced him to lie to the rest of the nation: a rise in ghoul births in the region, which is why the dead are piled out on the mound and not buried. When outsiders came, they pulled him out of his prison, cleaned him up and had him keep their secret, and they threatened his daughter. He looks around, asks where she is, and goes asking people.

Malek finds the mother he fed. "I gave you food earlier. Now, if ever there was a favour to be repaid, I need to know where your tavern is." It is that building over there, but it has been dry for some time. The DM spends a Fear for it. "You are the one who gave us the food. Thank you, sir." She says some people have been hiding food from the Blackheart out in the woods, and some families banded together and got away. Her cousin and his boy went for help, and everyone's rations were cut for a week after they disappeared. They are the survivors Zhar met in the woods. She asks whether the Blackheart are gone, and Malek says they have taken care of all of them. Her name is Jill. She says the magistrate does not know yet: his daughter was killed. She asks whether the party is leaving. Not yet, and they hope to get things running well enough to reestablish the Nightline. "That thing's been wrecked for over a decade now." The mine has the resources to fix it. "Maybe we can start a trade again. Go back to harvesting this forest." She goes to check on her boy.

Malek gives Yorg a ration. "You've done enough for me. I don't even know you." Malek searches Hark (Investigation 12) and finds a set of keys, 10 gp, and the thing Hark was reaching for: a small device with a pin. The tavern has been converted into a Blackheart headquarters. Malek ransacks it (Investigation 11) and finds an iron saddle box, a payroll box holding six silver bars. Every bottle is empty. He uses his sword as a crowbar to pry up the floorboards, finds one more empty bottle underneath, and the crowd outside backs away from the racket of breaking glass and cursing. Malek gives his share of rations to two exhausted miners.

### Scene 12: Time and Distance (~04:28--04:53)

Caly sits on the edge of a building and goes unnaturally still, watching the town. It is heartbreaking and rewarding at once: they are free. Yorg sits down next to her. "What you gonna do with these people?" Let them live, she says, and bring the Nightline back so they can trade and prosper. "You know you tell anyone about that place, this will turn into the biggest war you've ever seen." Caly admits they cannot carry any of it out and there is nobody to guard it. They have to get back to Fort Ashby and tell the acting marshal.

Yorg has heard of a road over the pass southwest of here that drops down into the Badlands. The miners came out through the mountains, which is dangerous: giants with a whole city, and small mechanical creatures near a large crater. One of their escorts had to trade away tools from Copper Waystation to a giant. Down in the Badlands you never know whose backyard you are stepping into. He and his boy travelled those roads, worked a few caravans, trading mostly.

"You're new at this, aren't you?" He tells Caly to let it take her and destroy everything in her path, and just try not to point it at the wrong person. The way to deal with that is time and distance: keep away from those you care about. "You ain't got that problem anymore." He asks what it is like besides the rage. Caly says it is strange to go from needing breath and sleep to needing neither. Yorg: "I give in. I just stop caring. The only thing I care about is my boy." She points out that he raged in the mine and never hurt a miner. He hurt the Blackheart plenty, and the miners stayed away from him. Caly's problem is the people she travels with: once the right people are dispatched, she does not want to turn the rage on her friends. It has happened every time. "They're still here, aren't they? He asks what Fort Ashby is like, a place for misfits with anger issues like them. Caly tells him about the local bar, owned by a one-armed orc named Grady. Yorg has heard the name: one of those Fang boys, the local leader at least. The story is that Grady won the place in a game years ago, the whole town, the Mercy House, the whole region. Caly did not know. She tells him she, her sister and their Pops all live there, and they all make things. Yorg and his boy came up from the south through the Red Valley, living place to place, and learned their trade in Ixaltan: pumps, mostly. They were heading to meet his cousin at Dustwater, who runs the reservoir there. Caly (History 21) knows the reservoir is famous, though she has never seen one in the Iron Marches, and that Pops has mentioned an inventor in Dustwater. His cousin is a hydrologist. Fort Ashby struggles with water, Caly tells him. If he can get pumps working and talk to his cousin about a reservoir in Ashby, the town will flourish. It would be a good place for him and his boy to settle down. "The only thing that calms me down." "Tinkering calms me down." She and her sister came over from Ixaltan about 15 years ago to live with Pops.

Caly tells Rhiaah about Yorg's pumps and his cousin's reservoir, and that Grady owns the whole town. Rhiaah has known that for a while. Of course she has. Rhiaah says that is why she quit working for Grady: she always had to wash dishes. Yorg asks about the warhammer, and the party tells him the Modron came with them down from an ancient floating city, where the hammer came from, forged by the god of war. The DM notes they have heard him named three ways: Awu by Quessa Merrow in the bar, a giant word for "war king" in the giants' history, and Ares in the Axiom. Yorg: "Live long enough, that'll happen. It's uncommon for ones as young as you."

Yorg starts rounding people up so the town can decide what happens next. Zhar tells him the iron price was paid by his son, and they will see him back to Kenneth.

### Scene 13: What Now (~04:53--05:23)

The square has calmed, but people are looking around for who will take charge. The magistrate sits despondent: his daughter is dead, and he spent 10 years in captivity. Zhar stays in his rooftop perch. "Not my monkey, not my circus." Malek goes looking for him (Investigation 5), finds a window ledge, and sits with his legs over the edge. Zhar's passive notices him. When Zhar leans over and speaks, Malek falls 15 feet out of the window for 9 damage and stays on the ground. "I fucking hate this place. I wanna go home."

With the elf rolling in the dirt, Rhiaah decides it is the wizard's job. "We are not here to recapture this town or take any of you. We are here to help. We just need to know what you want out of this situation." One of the miners says he has worked mines before, and this one is creepy but not that dangerous. Yorg pipes up: they have all heard of people fighting over water and mine rights, and what is in that place is far more dangerous. Every nation on this rock wants it, and it sits right on the border of the Pale Dominion. Rhiaah adds that it also holds a promise for Cross Hollow: it could fix the Nightline, and Pops already has plans (Persuasion 21, advantage). "If we keep this quiet, you can see the Nightline repaired." Rhiaah offers the 350 gold to buy provisions in Fort Ashby and bring them back, and invites some of the able-bodied to ride along.

A man at the back of the crowd, a little better fed and less gaunt, asks pointed questions. Zhar (Insight, dirty 20) and Malek (Insight 9) watch him. He is guarded and gathering information, but not malicious. "You will just leave us be. You'll send back your provisions. You'll help these people. What do you get out of it?" Rhiaah tells him she gave the dwarf an artifact worth more than half the lower Iron Marches, and money is no object to a Nox Rider. A working Nightline would be good for Fort Ashby and every other city, so 300 gold is a pittance. She adds 200 of her own. It is also selfish: "Me and my sister came halfway across this world to be able to see that train go." Her sister is a mechanic and well versed in repairs (Persuasion 14).

The man glances at two people standing apart in the crowd. "We have a supply of provisions, a cache out in the woods. It's not much, but it would give this town a few more days until you can return with help." His name is Maurice, and Rhiaah introduces herself as Luna. He is a hunter. Floyd kept the town's food locked up, so Maurice skimmed from the surrounding woods instead. Caly offers to repair the hunters' tools and weapons. Maurice addresses the crowd: many of them know him and have hunted with him, and they should hear these people out. "Boys, go dig it up." The two he glanced at head into the woods in two different directions and come back with a satchel of food each, mostly dried meats, a few vegetables and berries. All three are rangers, used to living off the land. People bring out the bits they hid from the Blackheart and pool what they looted from the crates. Caly sets up her smithing tools and starts a line of everything that needs repair. The magistrate sits staring into space. The town has been under siege for the better part of 15 years.

Zhar asks Maurice about the refugees. They headed over the range and crested the peak yesterday, last he saw. His boys can fetch them back in a day or two. Maurice points out a girl sitting alone, the daughter of the survivors who left over the range from Wolfshade Camp. He could not get her out with them, but he has kept her fed. He does not want Rattlespur back: they never did anything for this town, saw it as a means to an end, and wrote it off when travel got hard. The people here look out for each other. Zhar says he is not accustomed to town ownership. Where he is from, you either survive or you do not. "I can relate."

Maurice finds the black woods comforting, though outsiders look at them in awe. He warns them off the willow-like trees: there are more like the one in the gully, and they eat the dead, which is part of why revenants seldom come here, no offense. The revenants that do come are calm enough with him. Caly realizes she is not stressed at all. Most of the people here are outcasts and pariahs who came up into these hills, brought their families, and stayed. "Don't have to worry about much out here. Well, other than the occasional bandit tribe and sudden mine." Zhar: "At least you do not have dinosaurs." "If they did, they'd be zombie dinosaurs."

Maurice offers his place around the corner for sleep and climbs onto a roof to keep watch toward the mound, two rangers at either end of town. Caly works through the night repairing the town's tools. The townsfolk give the party a place to stay, a full belly and a place to rest.

### Scene 14: Wrap-Up (~05:23--05:29)

The party wants the Modron to help in the mine (D40 can talk), at least until someone who knows how to repair it comes. Nobody from town mines until food arrives. Caly takes three ghost iron ingots to work with. Yorg keeps the warhammer. There is no booze to be bought anywhere in town, even for a silver bar: the Blackheart have held it for years, and for the last several weeks Burnside and the regular shipments have not come. Burnside left with six carts of worked ghost iron and never came back. The party goes straight back to Fort Ashby next session, foraging on the way. Zhar has enough salve ingredients for five kits. Malek keeps the pace because his house has good bottles behind a shelf. Rhiaah is taking Fireball.
